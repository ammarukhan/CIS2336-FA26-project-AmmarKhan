    About the project

    A space designed for students

    The UH Sugar Land Student Center is a proposed gathering place for studying, collaborating, and building community.

    More than a place to study

    Our vision is a welcoming student center that supports the everyday needs of students who attend classes at UH Sugar Land. Whether you need a quiet corner between lectures or a shared space for a group project, the planned center would make it easier to connect and get things done.

    This website is a CIS 2336 coursework prototype for that proposed center. Services and policies shown here are illustrative, not an announcement of a real facility.
